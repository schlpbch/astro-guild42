Architects Aren’t the Smartest People in the Room.But they make everyone else smarter! It’s a common misconception that architects should make complex and difficult to reverse decisions. In reality, though, development teams are closer to the technical details and have to live with the consequences of their decisions. If teams are better equipped to make decisions, then architects help them make better decisions, for example by widening the solution space, unearthing hidden assumptions, or highlighting trade-offs.

This talk shows architects how they can make the most impact by not making decisions. **The presentation this evening will be given in English.**

## Speaker

We are proud to present [Gregor Hohpe](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ghohpe/), the world-famous elevator operating architect, whose home on the web is [architectelevator.com](https://architectelevator.com/). He is the author of the best-selling book "[Enterprise Integration Patterns](https://www.enterpriseintegrationpatterns.com/)", "[Cloud Strategy](https://architectelevator.com/book/cloudstrategy/)", "[Platform Strategy](https://architectelevator.com/book/platformstrategy/)" and "[The Software Architect Elevator](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-software-architect/9781492077534/)". As thought leader in the field of software architecture for over two decades he has been sharing his insights and experiences through his books, talks, and workshops. He is known for his ability to distill complex architectural concepts into practical advice that can be applied in real-world scenarios.

Gregor worked at several IT consulting firms before joining [Google](https://www.google.com/) as a developer in 2005. From 2012, he held multiple roles as an IT architect at [Allianz Insurance](https://www.allianz.com/). Five years later, he became Technical Director in Google's CTO office. In 2019, the [Government of Singapore](https://www.smartnation.gov.sg/) appointed Hohpe as an advisor for their Smart Nation initiative. From 2020 to 2024, he worked at [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/), most recently in the role of Senior Principal Evangelist. Now, he is a freelance consultant and speaker. Additionally, he is a member of the [IEEE Software Advisory Board](https://www.computer.org/csdl/magazine/so).
